01 About Us

Architecture isn't just what we do —
it's who we are.

Founded in Berlin in 1987, Meridian Construction Group began with a singular conviction: that every structure should be a testament to craft, precision, and purpose. What started as a small firm with bold ambitions has grown into one of Europe's most respected construction enterprises.

We approach each project as a partnership — listening deeply, engineering rigorously, and delivering relentlessly. Our portfolio spans commercial towers, residential masterpieces, industrial complexes, and heritage restorations, each bearing the unmistakable mark of Meridian quality.

Precision

Every measurement, every material, every moment — engineered to exacting standards.

Endurance

We build for generations, not quarters. Longevity is our benchmark of success.

Integrity

Transparent processes, honest communication, and accountability at every stage.
